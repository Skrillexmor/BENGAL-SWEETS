THE BENGAL'S STORY

Over 35 years of uncompromised purity, artisanal mastery, and sweet memories across two premier destinations in Rajkot.

Since 1989 • Rajkot Tradition

35+ YEARS OF ARTISANAL SWEET MAKING

Founded in 1989, Bengal Sweets (Bengal's) set out with a simple promise: to bring the delicate nuances of authentic Bengali Chenna sweets and royal Saurashtra penda to Rajkot families.

Led by Miral Patel, every single tray of Cham Cham, Mava Penda, and Kaju Katli is prepared without artificial stabilizers or shortening. We slow-simmer whole cow milk, churn pure Desi Ghee in-house, and select hand-picked Iranian pistachios and Kashmiri saffron.
